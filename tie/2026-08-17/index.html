#2 WATCH Safety & governance

Isolation and governance pressure rises for agent products

Medium impact · Well sourced — 1 company, 1 press

Anthropic has publicly described its cross-product isolation approach, while media outlets also reported that OpenAI disbanded the team responsible for assessing severe model risks.

Why this mattersProducts for executable tasks should include permission scope, evidence of actions and human interruption points in the default experience. Do not leave security guidance at the documentation level: high-risk tool calls need testable release gates and audit records.
