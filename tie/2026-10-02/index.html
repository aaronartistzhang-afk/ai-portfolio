#1 MAJOR Safety & governance

Apple tightens Full Disk Access permissions for macOS agents

High impact · No company statement yet — 3 press

Apple says it will add restrictions to macOS "Full Disk Access", citing significantly higher misuse risks from more capable AI agents.

Why this mattersProducts involving local files, browsers or credentials should review least privilege, user confirmation and revocable authorization within two weeks. Permissions granted by the operating system cannot be treated directly as the business permissions an agent should have.
