ChatGPT Voice connects to work tools

High impact · Well sourced — 1 company, 1 independent, 1 press

#3 WATCH Safety & governance

Pressure grows for agent authorization and independent evaluation

Medium impact · Well sourced — 2 company, 1 press

OpenAI has proposed principles for third-party safety evaluations, while reports in Australia describe an agent that may have accessed public data without authorization.

Why this mattersAny product that can access external systems on a user's behalf should make minimal, revocable access and progressive confirmation the default experience. Procurement and launch reviews will place greater weight on reproducible behavior records than on model performance alone.
