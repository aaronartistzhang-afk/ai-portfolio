#1 MAJOR Agents and tooling

Long-running multi-agent operation enters the cost engineering stage

High impact · Well sourced — 3 company, 1 independent

OpenAI demonstrated cross-code-task and ten-thousand-scale long-running agent operation, while Cohere reported that team orchestration can significantly reduce costs.

Why this mattersInclude task success rates and total cost per completed task in release thresholds. Products need to explicitly provide budgets, checkpoints, human takeover, and replayable traces. Differences in long tasks come more from orchestration and runtime control than from individual model calls.

#3 WATCH Hardware & infra

Microsoft advances multi-gigawatt deployment of Maia inference chips

Medium impact · No company statement yet — 1 independent, 1 press

Microsoft said Maia 200 is in production and plans to use Maia 300 to support multi-gigawatt inference deployments.

Why this mattersCloud products should avoid tying performance and cost assumptions to a single GPU path, and retain benchmarking and routing capabilities for heterogeneous hardware. Competition among inference providers will gradually affect the latency, throughput, and pricing that can be committed.
