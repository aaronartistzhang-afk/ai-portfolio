Siri opens third-party app actions

Medium impact · No company statement yet — 3 press

Frontier model companies face pressure on compute economics

Medium impact · No company statement yet — 1 independent, 2 press

Multiple reports focus on Anthropic's rapid revenue growth alongside widening losses, as well as its long-term compute contract commitments.
